# Additional free cost evidence

Two additional primary papers were screened after the [ten-case audit](external-cost-evidence-2026-09-07.md). Neither adds a matched independent full-cost observation. The total remains **zero**, and empirical MAPE remains unestimated. This bounded extension does not establish that suitable records do not exist.

| Additional paper | Verified evidence and permitted interpretation | Why it is not a matched observation |
|---|---|---|
| Gkika and Kyzas, *Cost Evidence Yields the Viability of Metal Oxides Synthesis Routes*, ACS Sustainable Chemistry & Engineering 13(41),17370–17379 (2025), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1021/acssuschemeng.5c06752) | Crossref HTTP200 confirms title, authors and bibliographic fields. Publisher-indexed full text describes laboratory-scale activity-based costing and total cost of ownership for titanium dioxide, alumina and ceria; precursor quantities/yields come from protocols and reported sources. Prices combine quotations, public/proprietary databases and expert input. | Independent costing approach is relevant methodological context, but the reported synthesis economics are not an independently observed, matched industrial production ledger. No proprietary input prices were imported or inferred. |
| Ferdous et al., *Techno-economic and life cycle analyses of the synthesis of a platinum–strontium titanate catalyst*, Catalysis Science & Technology 15,4419–4429 (2025), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1039/d5cy00189g); [free DOE copy](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/2588396) | Crossref HTTP200 confirms the citation. The DOE PDF was retrieved HTTP200 and read. Its abstract and methods explicitly use CatCost for TEA and R&D GREET for LCA, scaling laboratory synthesis into a proposed manufacturing scenario. | A separate publication using the same underlying costing method is not an independent actual-cost validation of that method. No modeled cost or scale-up assumption was reclassified as an invoice. |
