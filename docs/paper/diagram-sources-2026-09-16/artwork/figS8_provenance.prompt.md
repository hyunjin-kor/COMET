# Figure S8 concept: source-linked preparation record (text-free)

Flat, minimal scientific illustration for a journal figure. Pure white background. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, labels, logos, or watermarks anywhere; any documents must show only abstract gray lines instead of writing. Wide landscape composition, aspect ratio 3:1.

Show a left-to-right flow of three items connected by thin dark arrows:
1. Left: an open scientific journal article (two pages) with one short paragraph highlighted in a muted teal bar, and a small magnifying glass over it.
2. Center: a structured record card (a form with several rows of fields); one row is marked by a small pencil icon, indicating a user modification, and another row carries a small link icon.
3. Right: a stacked bar chart icon representing a cost breakdown, next to a small circular seal or stamp icon representing a verified checksum.

Style: clean vector-like line art with thin dark gray outlines, simple geometric shapes, soft flat fills, one muted teal accent color and light gray, no gradients, no 3D shading, no photorealism, no people. Generous empty space between items. Suitable for a scientific publication.
